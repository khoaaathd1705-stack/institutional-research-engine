## 03  |  Bulk Sales Strategy and Value Creation in Master-Planned Communities

**[Alex Fa – CFO & CIO, Vinhomes]**:
"To explain what drove this strong revenue recognition in 9M26: our bulk sales strategy contributed substantially to our top-line and earnings delivery. Bulk sales represent a core structural pillar of our Master-Planned Community (MPC) model.

As Vinhomes develops massive integrated townships spanning hundreds of hectares, there is extensive interest and participation from institutional sub-developers.

The bulk sales model provides several distinct strategic advantages:
1. It enables earlier revenue recognition and accelerated upfront cash collection.
2. Bulk transactions typically account for a higher proportion during the initial development phases of a township, while retail sales expand in share as the community reaches a mature, cruising stage.
3. For Vinhomes as master developer, bulk partnerships mitigate long-term development risk, optimize capital turnover, enhance blended profit margins, and diversify product concepts for residents.
4. Most importantly, large-scale bulk transactions validate deep market confidence from both international and domestic institutional developers in Vietnam's property fundamentals and in Vinhomes' execution capabilities.

Looking at historical benchmarks, 9M26 was the strongest nine-month period in Vinhomes' corporate history. Adjusted revenue reached VND 232.6 trillion, surpassing both FY2025 full-year revenue and our five-year historical average. Adjusted gross profit stood at VND 119.2 trillion, and EBITDA reached VND 111.4 trillion (three times 9M25) with an EBITDA margin of 47.9%."
