[Institutional Research] – IMP Analyst Meeting Update (9M26): Earnings Update and Strategic Outlook

Part 1: 3Q26 and 9M26 Financial and Operational Performance
Revenue Scale & September Rebound Dynamics:
- 3Q26 Net Revenue: Recorded at VND 544 bn (-5% YoY and -3% QoQ). Cumulative 9M26 gross revenue reached VND 1,961 bn (-7% YoY, achieving 61% of full-year guidance), while 9M26 net revenue stood at VND 1,652 bn (-8% YoY). Revenue performance was primarily weighed down by the contraction in the traditional retail pharmacy channel (OTC).
- September Rebound Signals: Operational performance exhibited a clear recovery in September 2026, with net revenue reaching VND 198 bn (+5% MoM), EBITDA reaching VND 55 bn (+49% MoM), and profit before tax (PBT) rising to VND 48 bn (+62% MoM), establishing operating momentum heading into the peak fourth quarter.

Profitability, EBITDA & Margin Expansion Trends:
- Profit Before Tax (PBT): 3Q26 reached VND 105 bn (+4% YoY, -11% QoQ). Cumulative 9M26 PBT reached VND 326 bn (+5% YoY), fulfilling 65% of full-year guidance.
- EBITDA: 3Q26 stood at VND 127 bn (-3% YoY, -10% QoQ). Cumulative 9M26 EBITDA reached VND 401 bn (flat 0% YoY), fulfilling 63% of full-year guidance. 9M26 depreciation, amortization, and interest expenses were recorded at VND 86 bn (-7% YoY).
- Gross Profit Margin (GPM): Maintained at high levels, reaching 41.0% in 3Q26 (improving from 39.6% in 3Q25) and 41.5% in 9M26 (compared to 40.0% in 9M25). Margin improvement was driven by an increased revenue contribution from high-margin EU-GMP products and optimized batch production planning.
- Net Profit Margin (NPM): Reached 17.3% in 3Q26 (vs. 13.4% in 3Q25); 9M26 NPM stood at 16.3% (vs. 14.2% in 9M25).

Cost Discipline: COGS Optimization and SG&A Rationalization:
- Cost of Goods Sold (COGS): 9M26 COGS dropped -11% YoY to VND 967 bn (from VND 1,085 bn in 9M25). The reduction in COGS (-11%) outpaced the decline in top-line revenue (-7% to -8%), as Imexpharm implemented large-scale campaign-based manufacturing schedules to minimize line changeover expenses and material losses. 9M26 gross profit reached VND 685 bn (-4% YoY).
- API Sourcing Management & Logistics: Global Active Pharmaceutical Ingredient (API) prices exhibited divergent movements depending on specific molecule supply-demand dynamics. As Imexpharm proactively locked in long-term contracts and maintained safety inventory buffers through 3Q26, international API price volatility exerted minimal impact on production costs. Blended portfolio API prices are projected to rise only marginally in 4Q26 and 1H27. Primary logistics variations were actively managed by rerouting procurement toward cost-effective freight routes.
- SG&A Rationalization: Total 9M26 SG&A expenses fell -8% YoY to VND 362 bn (representing 21.9% of net revenue), declining evenly across both functions. Selling expenses decreased -8% YoY to VND 231 bn (14.0% of net revenue) through stricter control of third-party service fees and a shift toward digital marketing and specialized online webinars. General & Administrative (G&A) expenses fell -8% YoY to VND 131 bn (7.9% of net revenue) due to disciplined administrative spending and the absence of a one-off ~VND 20 bn contract termination penalty incurred in 2025.

Manufacturing Plant Performance & EU-GMP Production Contribution:
- EU-GMP Manufacturing Contribution: The three EU-GMP certified facilities (IMP2, IMP3, IMP4) generated VND 1,222 bn in revenue, contributing 62% of total 9M26 revenue and meeting the strategic target of maintaining EU-GMP share above 60%.
- IMP4 Facility (Binh Duong): 9M26 revenue increased +20.7% YoY to VND 216 bn, raising its contribution to 11% of total revenue (up from 8–9% in 2025). Production volume surged +36% YoY, reflecting strong market demand for high-tech EU-GMP injectable antibiotics.
- IMP3 Facility: Contributed VND 715 bn in revenue (36% of total revenue). Despite a temporary shutdown of the Penicillin line during February and March 2026 for scheduled maintenance ahead of the EU-GMP recertification audit, 9M Penicillin production volume grew +3% YoY. The Cephalosporin line recorded a -27% YoY volume decline.
- IMP1 & IMP2 Facilities: IMP1 (WHO-GMP) generated VND 755 bn in revenue (39% of total revenue, volume down -11% YoY); IMP2 (EU-GMP) generated VND 291 bn (15% of revenue, volume down -12% YoY).
- Total Company Production Volume: Declined -12% YoY as management deliberately calibrated manufacturing throughput to align inventories with retail market absorption, prioritizing injectable production capacity.

Channel Analysis: Hospital Tenders (ETC) vs. Retail Pharmacy (OTC):
- Hospital Tender Channel (ETC) – Third-Quarter Recovery: 3Q ETC revenue grew +10% YoY and +12% QoQ, elevating hospital contribution to 57% of 9M26 total revenue (up from 54% in 9M25). Cumulative 9M26 ETC revenue reached VND 1,147 bn (-2% YoY), fulfilling 67% of full-year guidance.
- Hospital Bidding – Sustained Leadership in Tier 2: Imexpharm maintained its leading position in the Tier 2 hospital tender segment with winning bid value reaching VND 1,797 bn (+1.5% YoY), commanding a 15.2% nationwide market share. In Tier 1 and Tiers 3–5, the company participated selectively with win values of VND 119.2 bn (0.5% share) and VND 139.6 bn (0.5% share), respectively, to protect operating margins. Total 9M market tender openings reached: Tier 1 at VND 26.4 tn; Tier 2 at VND 11.8 tn; Tiers 3–5 at VND 26.4 tn.
- Retail Pharmacy Channel (OTC) – Restructuring and Chain Store Expansion: 9M26 OTC revenue declined -11% YoY to VND 831 bn (achieving 61% of target) due to a -25% drop in traditional retail pharmacies under stricter tax inspections and mandatory e-invoicing compliance. Conversely, modern pharmacy chains expanded rapidly: 3Q chain revenue surged +206% YoY (+15% QoQ); 9M chain sales grew +51% YoY, elevating chain share to 22% of OTC revenue (up from 13% in 9M25). OTC internal mix shifted to: Traditional retail at 63% (from 75%), Wholesales at 15% (from 13%), and Modern Chains at 22%.

Product Portfolio, R&D Pipeline & International Marketing Authorizations (MAs):
- Product Portfolio Mix: Antibiotics accounted for 72%, while Non-antibiotics represented 28% of revenue. Within non-antibiotics, digestive remedies grew +46% YoY (4.2% share), cough medicines accounted for 10.4%, antipyretics and analgesics 3.6%, anti-inflammatories 3.0%, dietary supplements 3.3%, anti-tuberculosis 2.1%, antiallergy 1.0%, and vitamins 0.9%.
- R&D Pipeline: IMP maintained 127 ongoing projects, initiated 20 new projects, and commercialized 10 new SKUs in 9M26.
- International Marketing Authorizations (MAs): Granted 01 new MA for lyophilized powder for injection in Singapore, alongside 01 injectable MA and 02 oral powder for suspension MAs in Malaysia, establishing foundation for export expansion across ASEAN.

Regulatory Environment & Healthcare Policy Shifts:
- Intensified Tax Inspections and Prescription Enforcement: Regulatory scrutiny over retail pharmacies has increased; the Government is finalizing four draft healthcare and pharmaceutical laws for submission to the National Assembly in October.
- National Pharmaceutical Database Deployment: Implemented under Official Letter No. 934/TTYTQG-DA dated August 12, 2026, from the Ministry of Health, requiring pharmaceutical businesses to register accounts and interconnect operational data. While creating short-term adjustment friction for small retailers, market transparency structurally benefits compliant, large-scale manufacturers with established digital infrastructure like Imexpharm.
- Policy Pivot Toward Disease Prevention and Chronic Disease Management: The Law on Disease Prevention and the Population Law (effective July 1, 2026) expand vaccination, early screening, and non-communicable disease management, supporting structural medium- and long-term demand for chronic disease medications.

Part 2: Strategic Outlook and Management Priorities
- FY26 Guidance Feasibility and Profitability Defense: With 9M revenue achieving 61% of full-year guidance and roughly two months remaining, management candidly acknowledged that meeting the full-year revenue target is no longer feasible due to the contraction in traditional OTC retail. However, the company remains focused on defending positive profit before tax growth (9M PBT has achieved 65% of full-year guidance) through strict COGS control and SG&A optimization.
- 2027 Growth Strategy – Centered on 3 Core Pillars: As retail pharmacies require additional time to adapt to regulatory changes and the IMP5 plant will not be operational in 2027, strategic priorities focus on:
  + Pillar 1: Concentrating resources on the hospital tender channel (ETC), leveraging EU-GMP quality advantages and market leadership in Tier 2 tenders.
  + Pillar 2: Maximizing throughput across existing EU-GMP facilities, specifically the IMP4 injectables plant and the IMP3 Penicillin line following scheduled maintenance.
  + Pillar 3: Deepening penetration into modern pharmacy chains and diversifying into non-antibiotic therapeutic categories (digestive, cardiovascular). Inpatient medical demand remains defensive, and expanding health insurance coverage for premium medications supports the substitution of expensive imports with domestic EU-GMP products.

Part 3: Investor Q&A Session
1. Will global API price trends impact IMP's COGS in 2027:
Management Response (Mrs. Le Thanh Hang – Executive Vice President & CFO): Global API prices have exhibited divergent trends depending on molecule-specific supply and demand balances. For Imexpharm, blended API costs across our portfolio are expected to increase only marginally. Because the company proactively secured long-term contracts and accumulated safety inventory buffers through 3Q26, international price movements have not materially impacted COGS. Fluctuations in maritime shipping freight rates represented the primary variable, which management mitigated by reallocating procurement toward optimized transit routes. Consequently, in 4Q26 and at least the first half of 2027 (1H27), API costs are not expected to exert upward pressure on COGS or create margin compression risks.

2. Why 9M26 ETC tender wins reached ~VND 1,800bn while recorded revenue was VND 1,147bn, and what channels will drive 4Q momentum:
Management Response (Mrs. Le Thanh Hang – Executive Vice President & CFO): Tender award values (~VND 1,800 bn in Tier 2) and recorded revenue (VND 1,147 bn) are distinct metrics separated by an operational time lag. Tender awards establish procurement ceiling frameworks spanning 1 to 2 years; actual call-off realization rates typically average 50% to 80% over the contract lifecycle. During 1H26, hospital call-off cadence slowed because subsequent tender cycles yielded lower award prices, creating administrative reluctance among hospital procurement boards to order against older, higher-priced contracts due to auditing justifications.
Regarding 4Q momentum, recovery drivers will stem primarily from: (1) Hospital tenders (ETC) entering peak clinical consumption and year-end budget disbursement periods (momentum emerged in 3Q with +10% YoY and +12% QoQ, continuing into August and September); (2) Modern pharmacy chains sustaining elevated growth (+206% YoY in 3Q, accounting for 22% of OTC revenue), offsetting traditional retail contraction; (3) Scaling high-value EU-GMP injectable output at IMP4 (+36% production volume) and normalized throughput on the IMP3 Penicillin line post-maintenance.

3. Management's strategy regarding free-float hovering near 10%, close to the minimum legal threshold under the Securities Law to maintain public company status:
Management Response (Mrs. Le Thanh Hang – Executive Vice President & CFO): Minority shareholder ownership currently stands at 10.1% of charter capital. Maintaining free-float at 10.1% was deliberately managed to ensure Imexpharm fully complies with Securities Law regulations and maintains its listing eligibility on the Ho Chi Minh Stock Exchange (HOSE), which mandates a minimum 10% voting share threshold held by non-major shareholders. Regarding potential equity offerings or liquidity expansion measures, the Board of Directors has no specific plans to disclose at this time. The transaction between SK and the new investor was a secondary share transfer between shareholders; Imexpharm's legal status, governance structure, and independent public listing remain completely unchanged.

4. Construction roadmap and commercial revenue timeline for the IMP5 facility:
Management Response (Mrs. Le Thanh Hang – Executive Vice President & CFO): The new IMP5 manufacturing complex project in Cao Lanh (Dong Thap) was initiated two years ago, and Imexpharm has fully disbursed all industrial land lease payments. Planning experienced a brief pause during the shareholder transition between SK and the incoming investor to review technical specifications. Total capital expenditure (Capex) disbursement for IMP5 is budgeted at approximately VND 500 bn. Designed under EU-GMP standards, the facility will specialize in chronic disease therapeutics (cardiovascular, diabetes, preventive therapies) to capture demand generated by the Law on Disease Prevention and Population Law. Commercial revenue generation is projected to commence around 2029–2030; within three operating years, IMP5 is expected to contribute 10% to 20% of Imexpharm's total revenue.

5. Future plans regarding cash dividend payout ratios:
Management Response (Mrs. Le Thanh Hang – Executive Vice President & CFO): Last year, Imexpharm distributed a 5% cash dividend alongside bonus shares. Over the next 2 to 3 years, backed by solid and stable operating cash flows, Management and the Board plan to progressively elevate cash dividend payout ratios to enhance shareholder returns. The company currently has no plans to issue new shares for equity capital raising.
