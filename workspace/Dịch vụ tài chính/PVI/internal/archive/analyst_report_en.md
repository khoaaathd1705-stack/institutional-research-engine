[Institutional Research] – PVI Holdings (PVI) Analyst Meeting Keynotes (9M2026): Performance Review & Strategic Outlook

Part 1: Financial Performance Review for 3Q26 & 9M26
Consolidated Top-line, Underwriting Profit & Capital Strength:
- Consolidated Revenue & PBT: Consolidated total revenue in 9M26 reached 26,277 billion VND (+15% YoY, completing 122% of the 9-month plan). Profit before tax (PBT) reached 1,749 billion VND (+18% YoY, completing 141% of the full-year target), setting a historical 9-month earnings record.
- Underwriting Profit & Technical Drivers: Net underwriting profit (KDBH) expanded to 947 billion VND (+25% YoY, completing 202% of the 9-month plan). Growth was driven by (i) strict underwriting quality resulting in a reduced loss ratio, and (ii) an actuarial methodology transition for unearned premium reserves (DPNV) and incurred-but-not-reported reserves (IBNR) from percentage-based allocation to exact daily pro-rata counting, contributing an estimated positive impact exceeding 200 billion VND to pre-tax earnings.
- Investment Income & Interest Rate Sensitivity: Net financial investment and other income reached 802 billion VND (+10% YoY, completing 170% of the 9-month plan), supported by a >20% portfolio size expansion and elevated deposit yields. Total bank deposits reached approximately 15,000 billion VND, yielding approximately 150 billion VND in incremental annualized pre-tax financial income for every 100 bps increase in deposit interest rates.
- Profitability & Operational Efficiency: Return on equity (ROE) reached 20.65% in 9M26 (compared to 17.92% in 9M25). The consolidated combined ratio improved to 86.0% (down 0.5 percentage points YoY). While full-year ROE is projected to normalize above 16% due to 4Q26 catastrophe claims seasonality and year-end operating expenses, it remains nearly 3 percentage points higher than 2025 levels.
- Capital Base Expansion: PVI Holdings expanded its charter capital from 2,342 billion VND to approximately 2,577 billion VND through a 10% stock dividend distribution, demonstrating balance sheet resilience without cash equity calls from shareholders over the past 15 years despite a tenfold expansion in total assets since 2011.

Core Operating Subsidiaries Performance:
- PVI Insurance (Non-Life Pillar): Total revenue reached 25,227 billion VND (+16% YoY, completing 124% of the 9-month target), serving as the primary growth engine. PBT reached 1,422 billion VND (+24% YoY, completing 146% of the full-year target), with ROE of 25.51% (vs. 25.15% in 9M25). Primary insurance via e-commerce reached 1,586 billion VND (up 1.55 times YoY), while inward reinsurance revenue reached 9,215 billion VND (104% of full-year plan), led by overseas inward reinsurance of 7,880 billion VND (+22% YoY). The loss ratio remained strictly controlled at 36.68% (compared to 38.16% in 9M25 and the 41.99% plan).
- Hanoi Re (Reinsurance Subsidiary): Total revenue reached 3,352 billion VND (+22% YoY, completing 121% of the 9-month target). Inward premium revenue reached 2,838 billion VND (+22.5% YoY) driven by treaty treaty agreements, with PVI Insurance contributing 51.4% and international/external partners contributing 48.6%. PBT reached 278 billion VND (+14% YoY, completing 136% of the 9-month plan, with underwriting profit completing 135.2% of the annual target). ROE reached 16.81% (vs. 15.32% in 9M25). The net retention rate stood at 48.02% (retained premium exceeded target by 368 billion VND), while the combined ratio reached 92.68% (net loss ratio of 47.22% and expense ratio of 39.69% vs. the 46.10% target).
- PVI AM & PIF Investment Fund: PVI AM generated 38.47 billion VND in revenue (112% of 9-month plan, -14% YoY due to reduced internal management fees) and 6.07 billion VND in PBT (-42% YoY following the closure of a legacy fund), while operating expenses were contained at 97% of budget. The PIF Fund generated 49.9 billion VND in realized profit (102% of 9-month plan, -48% YoY). An unrealized loss of 171 billion VND was recognized due to marked-to-market fluctuations of listed equities (SHB, KBC); however, these holdings are bound by structured contracts maturing in April 2028 (T4/2028), ensuring no impairment on fundamental earnings or cash flows.

Parent Company Dynamics & Capital Allocation Protocol:
- Standalone PVI Holdings Results: Standalone revenue reached 437 billion VND (101% of full-year plan), and standalone PBT reached 201 billion VND (103% of full-year plan). Standalone expenses rose by 1.9% YoY due to office leasing costs and headcount expenses, offset by reduced interest expenses.
- Root Cause of Standalone Divergence: The temporary decline in standalone top-line (-43% YoY) and PBT (-62% YoY) reflects an intentional capital allocation decision to retain earnings within PVI Insurance during the first 9 months of 2026. This strengthened PVI Insurance's solvency margin to support high-volume international reinsurance expansion, with subsidiary dividend payouts deferred to 4Q26. Normalized for quarterly dividend distribution, parent company 9M26 PBT would have grown +9% YoY. Full-year parent revenue remains on track.

3. Capital Allocation Hierarchy & Long-Term Dividend Policy – Question from Fund Managers:

4. Underwriting Discipline & Catastrophe Reinsurance Claims Dynamics – Question from Buy-Side Analysts:
Management Response (Ms. Nguyen Vu Tam - Deputy CEO, PVI Insurance):
- (i) Non-Price Competition: PVI Insurance strictly refrains from price-cutting to gain market share, maintaining underwriting discipline that resulted in a 36.68% loss ratio in 9M26.
- (ii) Reinsurance Recovery: The 80% rise in gross paid claims and 126% rise in reinsurance recoveries reflected the settlement of carried-forward 2025 typhoon and flood reserves (3 typhoons and 2 flood events in 2025) shifting into cash payments offset by international reinsurers.
- (iii) Liquidity Protection: PVI maintains 85%–87% of its investment portfolio in liquid bank deposits, ensuring immediate claim settlements without liquidity mismatch or reliance on timing of reinsurance cash inflows.

5. Hanoi Re Strategic Positioning & Strategic Investor Roadmap – Question from Sell-Side Analysts:

- (iii) Investor Criteria: PVI is actively evaluating potential global strategic reinsurance investors to enhance AM Best credit ratings (from ICR `Bbb+`) and global underwriting reach, while PVI Holdings firmly retains majority control.

6. Investment Portfolio Risk Governance & Equity Exposure (SHB/KBC & PIF Fund) – Question from Financial Analysts:

Holdings are tied to structured contracts maturing in April 2028 (T4/2028), ensuring contractually protected realization with zero impact on cash flow or corporate operational solvency.
